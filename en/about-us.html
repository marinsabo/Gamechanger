Mission, Goal & Vision

Mission

Game Changer has a mission to create a platform that will make a key contribution to the process of technological and digital transformation. Through a multidisciplinary approach to integrating progressive technological solutions in everyday life and work. By releasing the individual's potential and promoting technology - and creative solutions for their application, the Game Changer is the key bearer of the process of strengthening the capacity of individuals and organizations, to open new perspectives in business, the labour market and education. We see the game changer as a platform that has a revolutionary effect on the synergy of technology, capital and knowledge. A game changer is a bridge between different social groups: economic, educational, scientific and technological communities - with the end user.

Goal

The Game Changer platform aims to create a unique environment that connects digital and business leaders with the generations to come, as well as a professional orientation focused on the jobs of the future. Networking of decision-makers from different industries and connecting them with end users. The game changer takes responsibility for demonstrating practical applications through developing unique technological tools – on its own product development.

Vision

The game-changer vision directs us towards transforming the economy and education - towards the complete transition of society from analogue to digital. Creating an ecologically sustainable future in which technology is at the service of the development of humanity
