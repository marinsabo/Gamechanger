In line with the EU General Data Protection Regulation 2016/679 (GDPR), Game Changer is committed to protecting the personal data of its visitors and collecting only what is strictly necessary.

## What this website collects

This is a static website. **We use no cookies, analytics or tracking tools**, and fonts and images are served from the same server as the site — no third-party requests.

## Embedded video

The aftermovie is hosted on YouTube. It is loaded from `youtube-nocookie.com` **only after you press the play button**. From that point on, [Google’s privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) applies.

## External links

This site links to the websites of speakers, partners and media outlets. Their owners are responsible for their content and for how they process data.

## Contact

For any privacy questions, email us at [info@gamechanger.hr](mailto:info@gamechanger.hr). You have the right to access, correct and delete your data, and to lodge a complaint with the Croatian Personal Data Protection Agency (AZOP).
