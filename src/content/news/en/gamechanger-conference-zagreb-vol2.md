**The leading gaming and Web3 technology event in the region.**

We will bring the gaming industry, Web3, cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, e-commerce and advertising together to discuss how to catch the right wave with technology that is changing the world.

The Game Changer conference is the right place for anyone who wants to learn how to apply gamification, AI and blockchain technology in their business. There’s no better time to get ahead in the digital transformation game!

## For everyone, not the chosen few

Game Changer is not an elitist gathering. It brings together representatives of the IT industry, science, institutions and business communities, artists, students and individuals who want to take part in the dynamic transformation towards gaming, AI and Web3.

Game Changer is the first initiative that brings leaders from different industries and specialisations together in a dialogue about development.
