We are thrilled to share some great news with all of you: the Game Changer platform has signed an **exclusive strategic partnership with Ceres Blockchain Solutions**.

The agreement marks the start of combining expertise in blockchain technology and gaming with the market know-how we bring — to meet the business need for creating outstanding products.

The partnership grew out of months of joint work on a unique concept built on market needs, balanced with user needs and an excellent user experience.

We are proud that the design team includes exceptional people with vast experience in creating some of the world’s biggest game titles. The first video game is planned to launch next year.

This partnership will enable the development of the next generation of blockchain video games for a global audience. Our first product, called **W3B GAME**, will be the culmination of this collaboration.

Here’s to a great future together!
