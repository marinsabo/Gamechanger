Modern technologies, contemporary design and animation, NFTs and Croatian gaming projects are all part of the new **St.ART UP** festival, which focuses on modern technology and how it meets art.

Over four days, the festival hosts a range of activities and presents unique digital projects that put Croatia on the world map of art and technology.

The festival opens with an exhibition of digital works in design, animation and NFT technology at the French Institute (Preradovićeva 5). Innovation lovers can see work by seven progressive contemporary artists and studios: **Gamepires, Uptown Music Records, Pine Studio, KodaDot, Under the Stairs, Michelangelo Labs and Josip Vlah.**

## Schedule

- **24–27 May** — French Institute (Preradovićeva 5): exhibition of digital gaming works in animation, NFTs and design. Opening on Wednesday 24 May at 7 pm.
- **25 May (Thursday)** — Indie Expo
- **26 May (Friday), 6–7 pm** — _NFT & Blockchain_ workshop
- **27 May (Saturday), 11 am–3 pm** — French Institute: NFT fashion show during the city’s Saturday “špica”

Festival partners are Coca-Cola, De’Longhi, HGK, PPD Croatia, Wespa Spaces and D3 Stooges.
