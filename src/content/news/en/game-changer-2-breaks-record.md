Game Changer, the project set to put Croatia and Zagreb on the European map of digital destinations, has shown once again that the region has the know-how to answer the big question — where is the world we live in heading?

On 12 October, Zagreb’s Z Centar hosted the second Game Changer conference with more than 100 speakers. With its unique concept, Game Changer showed the enormous potential of connecting the gaming industry and Web3 with e-commerce, cybersecurity, artificial intelligence and marketing. More than 1,100 visitors heard about the latest trends first-hand from the people driving digital transformation — and took home inspiration for their own careers and businesses.

The conference opened with a networking dinner organised by the Odlučan 3 supercluster in cooperation with USAID, where blockchain experts from the region presented their work. Visitors connected in the large Investor lounge, tested partner products in the Expo zone and enjoyed an all-day DJ set — all rounded off with the _Change the Beat_ party.

## Applicable, new and useful

This year’s Game Changer 2.0 can be summed up in three words: **applicable, new and useful** — and by the fact that the share of women panelists rose from 17% to **34%**. Although women make up 27% of this industry’s workforce worldwide, the growing popularity of these careers in a traditionally male-dominated field was clearly reflected on stage.

Alongside Davor Bruketa, Sandro Mur, Roberto Barcellan, Gordan Giriček, Mislav Combaj, Vlaho Hrdalo, Jovan Tišma, Dražen Lučić, Tin Šarić, Vedran Vukman, Stipe Plejić, Aleksandar Gavrilović and many others, the speaker line-up featured many successful women building their careers in Croatia and abroad: Hajdi Ćenan, Ida Pandur, Ivanka Mabić Gagić, Dr Vanja Ljevar, Martina Silov, Belma Gutlić, Marijana Šarolić Robić, the sisters behind the design duo Boudoir and many more.

The halls were hosted by Sanda Mamić, Dino Oreški, Duje Stojak, Tomislav Pancirov and Nika Matičić, who led the startup stage. The win went to Timothei Jukić’s **Native Teams**. The jury was made up of Reno Budić, Ivona Atanasov Bandić, Katarina Ćosić, Bernard Ivezić, Dario Marčac, Filip Duvnjak and Ivan Ormuž.

The Expo zone was brought to life by Hanza Media, Samsung Electronics Adriatic, Coca-Cola, Addiko Bank, the European Commission, Crowe, Nestlé, De’Longhi, Philips, Podravka, RIT, Schneider Electric, Herbalife, LAQO, Revuto, Apis IT, PPD Global, Hrvatska Lutrija, HGSPOT, Papar and Miva wine gallery.
