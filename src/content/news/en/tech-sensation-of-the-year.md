Game Changer eCommerce & Fintech is the first major technology conference in Slovenia that brings e-commerce and fintech together. On **4 December 2023 at Cukrarna in Ljubljana** you will meet interesting people, discover new areas to grow your business, sharpen your skills and find inspiration.

Game Changer can be summed up in three words: **useful, advanced and innovative.**

Game Changer eCommerce & Fintech is the first conference on e-commerce and financial technology that promotes business opportunities through new chances for transformation. From the perspective of fintech and e-commerce, it focuses on every advanced technology field — AI, Web3, cybersecurity and marketing.

We bring together representatives of the tech industry, science, institutions and business communities, students and individuals who want to take part in the dynamic process of digital transformation. The full-day program runs across two halls of Cukrarna’s unique space, plus an Expo zone and Investor lounge, with more than **400 visitors** and over **40 panelists and speakers** expected. All in one day.

## Hall 1 — All about the change in e-commerce

Starting with AR and mobile shopping and social media as e-commerce tools, we move on to community building, the trends and challenges of first-party data collection, and the latest in packaging design and distribution. The hall closes with the panel _AI / next gen of eCommerce_.

## Hall 2 — The new game of fintech

Hall 2 opens with AI as a key transformation tool in fintech, followed by RegTech questions in Web3, the growth of alternative finance, embedded finance, SaaS trends and the new opportunities gamification brings to the fintech industry.

## After party

After the main program, attendees can try out our partners’ products and make new connections in the authentic atmosphere of the after party — with Santa Claus and live music. In just eight hours you’ll get something new and useful, with plenty of fun.

Special thanks to all the partners and friends helping Game Changer eCommerce & Fintech take its place on the technology map of Central Europe.
