The Boardroom Fintech & Ecommerce Forum is a new rising star in Central Europe. This year’s event will gather **more than 300 visitors** — and that’s just the beginning!

Fintech is one of the most dynamic areas of digital transformation. The expansion of Web3 and AI has added further acceleration that keeps reinventing fintech services.

At the same time, the video game industry is going through a global expansion that leaves its mark on most conventional industries — through gamification as a new approach to user acquisition, brand building, service improvement and loyalty.

**Boardroom by Game Changer** is the first fintech event to shine a light on industry trends through the decisive influence of Web3 and AI technologies and the application of gamification principles in building fintech products.
